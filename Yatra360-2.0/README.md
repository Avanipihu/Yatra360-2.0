AI-Powered Tourism Growth & Safe Mobility Platform
Not just where to go — how, when and why.

YatraSaathi is an AI-powered web platform designed to make travel easier, safer, and more personalized while helping local tourism businesses and authorities understand and manage tourist demand.

Unlike traditional travel platforms that mainly focus on bookings or popular attractions, YatraSaathi combines tourist preferences, weather, safety, accessibility, transport, crowd/demand and local tourism data to provide context-aware recommendations.

 What Makes YatraSaathi Different?
Our core idea is:

Turning Tourist Traffic into Tourism Growth.
Instead of sending every tourist toward the same popular destinations, YatraSaathi can identify suitable less-crowded and underutilized alternatives based on the traveller's preferences and current conditions.

This creates benefits for the entire tourism ecosystem:

Tourist → Better Experience → Local Business → More Opportunities → Smarter Tourism Planning

 Problem
Tourists often have to use multiple platforms for:

Tourist attractions
Hotels and accommodation
Food
Weather
Navigation
Public transport
Safety information
Emergency services
Local experiences
At the same time, popular tourist destinations can become overcrowded while lesser-known destinations and local businesses remain underutilized.

There is a need for a platform that connects tourist needs with real-world tourism conditions.

 Our Solution
YatraSaathi brings these different information sources together and uses AI to provide personalized travel recommendations.

The platform considers:

 Traveller type
 Travel date and duration
 Budget
 Interests
 Weather
 Estimated crowd/demand
 Safety
 Accessibility
 Transportation
 Food and local businesses
 Local attractions and experiences
The result is a personalized and context-aware travel plan.
